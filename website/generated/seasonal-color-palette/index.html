Frequently asked questions

What is a seasonal color palette?
It groups colors that harmonize with your natural coloring — skin, hair, and eyes — for flattering style choices.

Even indoor daylight works. Avoid heavy filters for the most accurate palette.
Can this help with makeup shopping?
Yes. Use your palette to shortlist foundation, blush, and lip shades.
How many seasons do you support?
We map you to classic seasonal families with modern, wearable swatch groups.
Is seasonal color analysis accurate?

Can I retake with different makeup?
