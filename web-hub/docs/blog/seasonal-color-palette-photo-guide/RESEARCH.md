# Research brief: seasonal-color-palette-photo-guide (2026-11-16)

Primary keyword: seasonal color analysis. Intent: informational plus how-to (what are the seasons, which am I, how do I do it from a photo).
Note: Google blocked automated SERP scraping and Bing returned unrelated results, so the competitor set was chosen from web search results and opened in Chromium. Screenshots: media/blog/seasonal-color-palette-photo-guide/research/.

## Competitors read (H1/H2 outline, words)
- Sterling Style Academy, Ultimate Guide to Color Analysis (~2900 words, no tables, no FAQ): what it is, 4 seasons, 12 subtypes, "science behind tools", myths. Vendor, sells courses.
- The Modest Man (~1800, no tables): 12 subtypes, undertone/eye/hair, best-okay-worst, fabrics, aging, "try AI". Subtype names differ from Sterling.
- Woman and Home (~4400): what colour suits me, skin tone, season by season, can colour change. No tables, no photo guidance.
- YouCam AI personal color analysis (~1100, 1 table): vendor pitch for its app.
- Wardrobe help docs 12 color seasons (~4800), Roberta Style Lee (~1900), My Style Box (~1300): palettes and subtype lists.

## Gaps
No competitor combines: honest evidence on whether seasons are scientifically valid; a photo-capture checklist grounded in light/white balance facts; a season-by-season table with makeup AND jewelry; a real-life draping test; limits. Most are vendor pages.

## Information-gain plan
Evidence box (Perrett and Sprengelmeyer 2021), photo checklist table, 3-check undertone method, 12-season table with note that names vary, draping steps, limits callout.

## Verified facts
- Itten 1928 class, 1961 The Art of Color linked subjective colors to four seasons; Kentner 1978; Caygill and Jackson 1980; seasons "to some degree, arbitrary". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_analysis (read 2026-11-16)
- Perrett and Sprengelmeyer, i-Perception, 15 Nov 2021: categories "not defined scientifically"; 12 faces of White women; N = 96 and 75; cooler blue for fair, warm orange/red for tanned. https://doi.org/10.1177/20416695211053361 (abstract read on SAGE) and https://news.st-andrews.ac.uk/?p=21144
- 12-season = hue, value, chroma, three subtypes per season (Sterling). Modest Man names Autumn True/Deep/Soft and Winter True/Bright/Deep.
- Undertone tests (veins, gold vs silver, white vs off-white, olive note): https://www.healthline.com/health/beauty-skin-care/skin-undertones
- Skin color melanin plus hemoglobin: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_skin_color
- White balance, skin tones judged critically: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_balance
- CRI max 100 = daylight spectrum; weak guide below 5000 K: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_rendering_index
- Color constancy: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_constancy ; simultaneous contrast: https://colourliteracy.org/simultaneous-contrast
## Not used (unverified)
Kawase, Adachi and Nagashima 2026 (cited by Wikipedia, paper not found); Perrett press-release participant count of 160 (conflicts with abstract); accuracy figures from vendors.
