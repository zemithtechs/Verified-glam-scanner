## Facts used and their sources
- Karras, Wang, Li, Kemelmacher-Shlizerman (Univ. of Washington + Google Research, SIGGRAPH 2026 listing): methods "largely overlook" fit; "default to generating well-fitted results regardless of the garment or person size"; "hallucinate garment fit". https://johannakarras.github.io/FIT
- Baytar et al., Journal of Fashion Marketing and Management (study done 2016, Cornell news 2020): AR mirror, one dress; size could be found, fit hard to judge at bust/waist/hips; style, fabric, touch, weight, comfort, fit differed; color was the only factor with no difference. https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2020/05/augmented-reality-can-improve-online-shopping-study-finds
- NRF press release (2025-10-15): 15.8% of 2025 sales, $849.9B returned; 19.3% of online sales; surveys of 2,006 consumers and 358 merchants. No breakdown by reason, so no fit-share claim made.
- FTC Online Shopping: site must say whether full refund; ask who pays return shipping, days, restocking fee; sale items often stricter.
- ASTM D5585-21 scope: body-measurement tables are a baseline considered with fabric type, ease, styling, fit.
- Song et al., arXiv 2403.08453: original clothing affecting try-on area is a known problem (basis for "wear plain, close-fitting").
- True Fit (vendor blog, flagged as vendor): four layers of size variation.
