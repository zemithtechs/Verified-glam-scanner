## Facts used and where they come from
- TikTok Support, About AI-generated content: AIGC definition names cartoons and anime; label requirement for realistic AIGC; AI face swap as a significant-edit example; auto labels from TikTok AI effects or C2PA Content Credentials, auto label can't be removed; misleading creator label breaks Terms of Service; bans likeness of under-18s and adult private figures without permission.
- YouTube Help, Disclosing use of GenAI content: disclose realistic content that makes a real person appear to say or do something they didn't; no disclosure for beauty filters, color or lighting filters, special effects filters, clearly unrealistic content; lists not exhaustive; video focus.
- Meta Newsroom (Apr 2024, updated): "AI info" label from industry-shared signals or self-disclosure; renamed from "Made with AI"; label for AI-edited-only content moved to the post menu. The page does not name C2PA.
- C2PA home and Content Credentials: "nutrition label for digital content". C2PA Explainer 2.2: tamper-evident (7.1.3), can be removed ("Yes it can", 7.2.3), cannot say whether content is true (7.2.2).
- arXiv 2605.24735 (preprint, May 2026): 420 apps identified, 155 tested, 70% of apps with face swap functionality have no technical safeguards against nude image generation. Abstract only read; not peer reviewed; affiliations not verified so not stated.
- FTC consumer alert, 1 Oct 2026 (US): Take It Down Act, platforms remove a reported explicit image of a child, deepfakes included, and known identical copies within 48 hours; fake explicit images of children cause real harm; report at TakeItDown.ftc.gov. Stated for the US only. Chromium and curl get blocked or 404 on this URL, WebFetch loaded it.
- CEOP Education (UK NCA): consent of the parent or carer and the child before posting other people's children; default settings can show posts to anyone; location data on photos.
