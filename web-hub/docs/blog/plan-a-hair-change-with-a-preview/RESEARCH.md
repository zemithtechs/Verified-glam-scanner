## Facts used (all read on the source page)
- AAD, coloring tips: stay within 3 shades of natural color; lightening more needs higher peroxide volumes and more damage; test store-bought color; rash/itching means allergic reaction.
- FDA hair dyes + Cosmetics Safety Q&A: patch test (elbow or behind ear, two days) every time; salons too; reactions can appear after years; negative skin test is not a guarantee.
- Live Science citing StatPearls: about 0.5 inch (13 mm) per month, 6 inches a year; anagen 2 to 6 years; about 3 months resting before shed.
- Cleveland Clinic (Dr Wilma Bergfeld): 50 to 150 hairs lost per day; warning signs; pull test; see a dermatologist.
- AAD female pattern hair loss: widening part, thinner ponytail; other causes look alike; dermatologist diagnosis first.
- Healthline coarse hair: coarse = strand width; thick = follicle density; two different things.
- Birnbaum et al., Skin Appendage Disord 2017: density means 214 to 230/cm2 (23 Caucasian), 169 to 178 (99 Hispanic), 148 to 160 (44 African descent), one NYC clinic sample. Used only to show density varies and is not a style label.
- Marie Claire (stylists Leal, Cleveland, Warren): maintenance is the top regret, trims every 2 to 3 months, fringe/layers hard to tie back, color after a big cut, ask how to style at home, consult first.
- L'Oreal: thick hair often suits longer cuts; straight hair carries blunt bangs, curly hair needs a routine; bring a visual example.
- Perfect Corp: result less precise with hair over forehead; fine hairlines, very curly hair, dim or angled photos look less natural (vendor statement).
